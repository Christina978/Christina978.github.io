# Final Review: Smart Glasses DE

## Overview
The `venture-team` workflow produced market research, positioning strategy, marketing copy, a QA-verified HTML prototype, and QA documentation for bringing smart glasses to the German market.

## Key Takeaways
- **Market Fit:** Germany favors practical utility, optical comfort, and clear privacy boundaries over novelty camera features.
- **Positioning:** Lead with Privacy-by-Design, transparent capture indicators, prescription/ergonomic readiness, and distinct Everyday/Optical vs. Enterprise/Field tracks.
- **Prototype Status:** Responsive prototype built and verified with zero remaining `🔴` QA issues.
- **Next Steps:** Validate messaging with German B2B/prosumer buyers and optical partners, and confirm GDPR/data-processing and prescription workflows for a pilot.
