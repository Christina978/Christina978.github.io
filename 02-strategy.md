# Positioning Strategy: Smart Glasses DE

## 1. Core Positioning Statement
For privacy-conscious German professionals, field technicians, and everyday eyewear wearers, **Smart Glasses DE** provides lightweight, prescription-ready AI and audio-visual eyewear engineered with **Privacy-by-Design, transparent capture indicators, and GDPR-aligned data controls**—delivering hands-free assistance without intrusive bystander surveillance or bulky hardware.

## 2. Target Segments
- **Segment A — Enterprise & Field Teams (Primary B2B Wedge):** Field service technicians, logistics coordinators, and inspectors needing hands-free checklists, live translation, and guided workflows with clear compliance boundaries.
- **Segment B — Everyday & Optical Wearers (Prosumer / Retail Wedge):** Tech-forward professionals and glasses wearers who want discreet audio/AI assistance, all-day comfort, and prescription-ready frames.

## 3. Key Value Pillars
1. **Privacy-by-Design & Transparency:** Clear visual/audio capture indicators, user-controlled recording states, and transparent processing disclosures tailored to German/EU expectations.
2. **All-Day Wearability & Prescription Fit:** Lightweight frame ergonomics and prescription-lens compatibility so it feels like real eyewear.
3. **Practical Hands-Free Utility:** Real-time translation, step-by-step task support, and quick summaries without pulling out a phone.

## 4. Differentiation & Guardrails
- Avoid framing the product around covert or public-space bystander capture.
- Explicitly distinguish between on-device/user-initiated actions and cloud/AI features.
