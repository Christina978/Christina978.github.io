In Google Gemini, skills package repeatable instructions and quality guardrails so you don't have to re-explain your workflow every time you start a new task. 
 
Skill: synthesize-research-sources 
This is is designed for multi-source research synthesis and source auditing—such as comparing studies, market or industry reports, articles, or reading notes on a shared topic.
When triggered, it guides Gemini through five consistent steps:
Scope the question and sources: Clarifies the central comparison question and catalogs each provided source.
Extract key dimensions consistently: Pulls uniform attributes across sources (objective, method, sample or scope, findings, and limitations).
Build a comparison matrix: Organizes sources side by side in a Markdown table and flags agreements, contradictions, or scope mismatches.
Synthesize themes and gaps: Groups takeaways across sources into core themes and highlights open questions or evidence gaps.
Verify source grounding: Checks claims against the supplied material and marks missing details as Not stated in source rather than guessing.
